About The Velvet Stage

Founded in 1987 by a drama teacher, a plumber and a very determined librarian, The Velvet Stage has staged over 120 productions — from Shakespeare in the round to pantomimes that sell out in a weekend. Everything is run by volunteers, and every penny goes back into the building, the costumes and the next show.
