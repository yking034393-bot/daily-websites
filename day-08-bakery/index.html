      Our Story

      The Golden Whisk started in 2019 with one second-hand oven, a bag of flour, and a stubborn belief: bread should take time. Our starter, "Goldie", is now six years old and still leavens every loaf we bake.

      We mill our own flour, laminate our croissants by hand, and never bake more than we can sell in a day — because yesterday's bread belongs to the birds, not to you.

      How a loaf is made

      1. Mix — flour, water, salt, and Goldie the starter.
      2. Rest — 36 hours of slow, cold fermentation.
      3. Shape — by hand, every single loaf.
      4. Bake — stone hearth, 240°C, steam and patience.
      5. Cool — at least an hour before it leaves the shelf.
