Plant care 101

Three habits that keep almost any houseplant alive.

💧

Water less, not more

Check the top 2 cm of soil first. If it is dry, water thoroughly until it drains — then leave it alone. Overwatering kills more plants than drought.

☀️

Match the light

"Bright indirect light" means near a window, out of direct sun. A plant stretching toward the window is asking for more light; scorched leaves mean less.

🪴

Repot when root-bound

Roots circling the bottom or poking out of drainage holes mean it is time for a pot 3–5 cm wider, with fresh, free-draining potting mix.
