Keep them blooming

  1. Trim stems at an angle under running water before placing in the vase.
  2. Fresh water daily — change it, and re-trim every two days.
  3. Keep cool — away from radiators, fruit bowls and direct sun.
  4. Remove spent blooms so the rest of the bunch lasts longer.
