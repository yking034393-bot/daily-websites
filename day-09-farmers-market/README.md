# Day 09 — Maple & Main Farmers Market

**Date:** 2026-11-13
**Topic:** Local farmers market website

## What it is

A warm, market-day website for a weekly farmers market on Maple Street,
helping visitors browse this week's stalls, see what's in season, plan
their Saturday morning, and reserve produce for pickup.

## Features

- **This week's vendors** — 16 stalls across produce, bakery, dairy & eggs,
  meat & fish, crafts and hot food, with live search, category filters, and
  sorting by name, stall number or price.
- **Basket** — slide-in drawer with quantities, running total, and a
  Saturday pickup reservation code.
- **What's in season** — month tabs (November, December, January) showing
  seasonal crops and which stalls carry them.
- **Market day schedule** — a timed timeline of the Saturday, from the
  8am bell to the 12:30pm "wonky veg" box.
- **Recipes** — three dishes built entirely from this week's stalls, with
  the stall named for every ingredient.
- **Sell at the market** — stall request form with validation and
  confirmation.
- **Visit** — address, transport, accessibility, and a Friday newsletter
  sign-up with email validation.
- **Responsive design** — mobile menu, stacked layouts on small screens.

## Files

- `index.html` — page structure and content
- `styles.css` — styling (green/cream market palette)
- `script.js` — vendor catalogue, basket, seasonal produce, forms
