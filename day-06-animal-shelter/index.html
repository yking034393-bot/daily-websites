How Adoption Works

1

Meet them

Visit the shelter or browse online, then spend time with the pet you're drawn to.

2

Apply

Fill in a short application. We check your home is a good fit — usually within 48 hours.

3

Take them home

Pay the adoption fee (from $50), sign the agreement, and welcome your new best friend.

Adoption fees cover vaccinations, microchipping, and spaying/neutering — and help the next animal waiting for a home.
