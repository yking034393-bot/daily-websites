# Day 05 — Pedal & Peak Cycles (Bicycle Shop)

**Date:** 2026-11-09
**Topic:** Bicycle shop

A friendly local bike shop website for "Pedal & Peak Cycles", a fictional shop in Hillcrest, Lakeside.

## What's inside

- **Hero** with a ride-of-the-month deal and shop stats
- **Features** — free first service, expert fitting, free local delivery, trade-in
- **Bike catalogue** — 12 bikes across road, mountain, hybrid, electric, and kids, with live search, type filters, and price sorting
- **Basket** — add bikes, with a basket counter and total summary toast
- **Workshop & services** — six services with prices and turnaround times, plus a working booking form
- **Rentals** — day-hire city, trail and e-bikes
- **Local trails** — three graded rides from the shop door
- **Visit us** — address, hours, and a newsletter sign-up form

## Files

- `index.html` — page structure
- `styles.css` — fresh, outdoorsy styling (deep teal, warm orange accent)
- `script.js` — catalogue data, search/filter/sort, basket, booking and newsletter forms, toasts

## Run it

Open `index.html` in any browser. No build step or server needed.
