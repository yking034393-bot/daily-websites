    How a piece is made

    1. 1. Wedge — knead the clay to remove air bubbles.
    2. 2. Throw — centre the clay and shape it on the wheel.
    3. 3. Trim — refine the foot and walls once leather-hard.
    4. 4. Bisque fire — first firing to harden the piece.
    5. 5. Glaze — dip or paint on colour and texture.
    6. 6. Glaze fire — the kiln works its magic at 1,220°C.
